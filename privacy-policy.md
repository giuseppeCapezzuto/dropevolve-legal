# Privacy Policy — Drop & Merge

**Effective date:** 2026-09-06
**Last updated:** 2026-11-24

Drop & Merge, formerly Drop & Evolve ("the app", "the game"), is developed by **GC Apps** ("we", "us").

Contact for any privacy question or request: **gcapplicationdev@gmail.com**

This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.
It is written to be read, not to be survived.

---

## The short version

- The app has **no accounts of its own and no real-money purchases.** We never
  ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise
  location.
- **Your progress is backed up to your own Google account, never to us.** If
  you use Google Play Games, your collection, coins and levels are saved there,
  so a new phone or a reinstall picks them up. Android's own backup may keep a
  copy of the save file in your Google account too. We never receive either.
- **Gameplay stats are anonymous totals, and you can switch them off.** The
  game counts things like "level 37 was lost 90 times today", with no
  identifier of any kind, so no number can be traced back to you or your
  phone. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland they are off until you say yes; on
  a new install elsewhere they start on. Either way, one switch in Settings
  turns them off.
- **Ideas you choose to send us.** Once, the game may ask what you would like
  added or improved. Only if you write an answer and tap Send, the text reaches
  us with your language, the app version and how far you have played. Nothing
  that identifies you is attached, and the question can be skipped.
- **No ad ever interrupts play.** Video ads are rewarded videos you tap to
  start; the only other ad is a small banner in a strip at the bottom of the
  screen.

## What the app collects

### 1. Anonymous gameplay stats

The game keeps **daily totals** of what happens in play, such as how many
times a level was won or lost and how many drops it took on average. Before
anything leaves your phone, the events of the day are added together into
these totals. What is sent contains **no identifier of any kind**: no
advertising ID, no device or installation ID, no account, nothing you typed.
Each total carries only the date, the app version and the game's language
(for example `de`, never your country).

**When they are sent.**

- **In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland** (or whenever the app cannot tell where
  you are), stats are **off** until you agree. The app asks you once you have
  won level 4, with two equal choices. If you decline, it asks again at most
  twice, six months apart.
- **Elsewhere, on an install that began with this version,** stats start
  **on**, without a question.
- **Elsewhere, on an older install,** nothing changes: stats stay off unless
  you said yes before or switch them on in Settings. The app does not ask.
- **Everywhere,** the **Analytics** switch in Settings turns them off at
  once, and what was waiting to be sent is deleted. A "no" you gave earlier is
  kept everywhere.

To decide which rule applies, the app uses Google's consent check (the same
one that governs ads, which looks at your IP address to see whether EU-style
rules apply) and your phone's region setting. If either points to Europe,
Europe's rules apply. Nothing about this decision is sent to us.

The totals cover these twenty-one events, and nothing else:

| Event | What it records |
|---|---|
| `level_start` | Which level was started, and which try at it this is |
| `level_complete` | Which level was completed, the star rating, and how many of its drops were used |
| `level_fail` | Which level was failed, the star rating, how many of its drops were used, and whether it ended on the drop limit, a full jar or the clock |
| `level_quit` | Which level was left or restarted before it ended, and how many of its drops were used |
| `creature_discovered` | Which creature tier and variant was discovered |
| `endless_game_over` | The run's score and your best score |
| `rewarded_shown` | That a rewarded ad was displayed, and which reward |
| `rewarded_completed` | That a rewarded ad was watched to the end, and for a free booster which one was picked |
| `rewarded_failed` | That a rewarded ad could not be shown |
| `rescue_used` | That a lost level or Endless run was rescued, and whether with in-game coins or a rewarded ad |
| `shop_purchase` | Which item was bought in the in-game shop, and its price in in-game coins (never real money) |
| `booster_used` | Which booster was used, and whether in a level or Endless |
| `notification_opened` | That a game notification was tapped, and which kind (farm, streak or comeback) |
| `day_active` | That the game was opened on a given day, at most once a day, how many days have passed since it was first opened on this device, and how many days in a row farm orders have been delivered, and whether the music and sound settings are on or off |
| `quest_claimed` | That farm quest rewards were collected, which kind (quest, streak gift or Daily Jar) and how many |
| `farm_build` | Which farm building was restored or upgraded, and to which stage |
| `farm_pet` | That a farm animal was petted, which kind of animal, and how many in-game coins it gave |
| `visitor_greeted` | Which farm visitor was greeted, whether for the first time, and whether it is a rare one |
| `album_set_claimed` | Which set of the farm's visitor album was completed and its reward collected |
| `decor_bought` | Which farm decoration was bought with in-game materials (never real money) |
| `decor_placed` | Which farm decoration was placed, and on which spot of the farm |

These totals contain only numbers and short internal labels. They contain no
personal information and nothing you have typed.

They are sent to our own small server, run on **Cloudflare**. Cloudflare
necessarily sees your IP address to deliver the request, as with any internet
connection; our server does not read or keep it, keeps no request logs, and
stores only the totals. Before each send, the app asks the server whether
collection is on; we can switch it off for every player at once.

We use the totals to see which levels are too hard or too easy, and whether
players come back. They cannot identify you and are never sold or shared. We
keep them only in this anonymous form and will never try to link them to a
person or a device.

### 2. Advertising — Google AdMob

The app shows two kinds of ad. **Rewarded videos**, which you always start
yourself by tapping an offer, and a **banner** in a fixed strip at the bottom
of the screen. No ad ever interrupts a run: there are no pop-up or full-screen
ads you did not choose.

To serve these, **Google AdMob** processes your device's **advertising ID**,
your **IP address**, and your interactions with the ad. Whether you see
**personalised** or **non-personalised** ads depends on the choice you make in
the consent form the app shows you.

Non-personalised ads still require the advertising ID for frequency capping and
fraud prevention, but are not selected based on a profile of your interests.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's
**Settings → Privacy → Ads**.

### 3. Your game progress — on your phone and in your Google account

The following is written to a save file inside the app's private storage on your
phone:

- Which creatures you have discovered, and their variants
- Your level progress, star ratings and best scores
- Coins, purchased cosmetics and boosters
- Your settings: language, haptics, sound, music, and your analytics choice

**It is never sent to us.** Two Google services can keep a copy **in your own
Google account**, so your progress survives a new phone or a reinstall:

- **Google Play Games.** If you are signed in to Play Games — most Android
  phones sign you in automatically, and you can also sign in from the game's
  Settings — the game saves your discoveries, levels, best score, coins,
  cosmetics and boosters there (never your settings). To sign you in, Play Games
  shares your Play Games profile with the game: your player ID, gamer name and
  avatar. Google also collects its own diagnostics to keep the service working.
  If you do not sign in, nothing is saved to Play Games and the game works
  exactly the same.
- **Android backup.** If backup is turned on for your phone, Android copies the
  save file (and only the save file) to your Google account and restores it when
  you reinstall. This follows your phone's backup setting, not ours.

### 4. Ideas you send us — only if you write one

After you have played a while, the game may ask once what you would like us to
add or improve, with two boosters as a thank-you. Answering is optional: "Not
now" and the back button skip it.

If you write an answer and tap **Send**, the app sends us:

- the text you wrote (at most 500 characters)
- the game's language, the app version, the furthest level you have cleared,
  and whether you had just finished a level or an Endless game

It is sent through Google Forms and stored in a Google Sheet that only we can
open. No advertising ID, app instance ID, Play Games ID or other identifier is
attached, so we cannot link an answer to you or your phone. **Please do not
write your name, email address or other personal details in it.** If you do,
we delete them when we read the answer.

### 5. What we never collect

No name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, calendar,
photos, camera, microphone, files, precise or GPS location, health data,
payment information, or anything else you type. The app has no chat, no
social features and no in-app purchases.

## Legal basis, and your rights under GDPR

GC Apps is established in Belgium, so the EU General Data Protection
Regulation applies to this app everywhere it is distributed.

**Legal basis.** Personalised advertising is processed on the basis of your
**consent** (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you give through the in-app consent
form, and which you may withdraw at any time without affecting the lawfulness
of processing before withdrawal. The gameplay stats are anonymous totals and
not personal data; in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland they are still sent only
with your consent, given through the in-app question or the Settings switch,
because reading information from your device needs it there. Saving
your progress to Google Play Games is processed to provide the cloud save you
use by signing in (Art. 6(1)(b)); you can stop it by signing out of Play Games.
An idea you send us is processed on the basis of your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)),
given by writing it and tapping Send.

**Your rights.** You have the right to request access to, correction of,
erasure of, or restriction of processing of your personal data; to object to
processing; and to data portability. Because the app holds no account of its
own and we receive no identifier that points to you as a person, we will usually need the advertising
ID from your device to act on a request about ads data. The gameplay stats
hold nothing that can be linked to you, so there is nothing in them to access
or erase for one person. Write to
**gcapplicationdev@gmail.com** and we will respond within one month.

**Withdrawing consent.** Open **Settings** in the app. The Analytics
switch turns them off immediately. The privacy options row reopens the ads
consent form.

**Complaints.** You may lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection
Authority — *Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des
données*, Rue de la Presse 35, 1000 Brussels — or with the supervisory
authority of the EU country where you live.

## Deleting your data

Drop & Merge has no account of its own, so there is nothing to close. To have
your data deleted:

1. **Delete what is on your phone.** Uninstall the app, or open Android
   **Settings → Apps → Drop & Merge → Storage → Clear storage**. This erases
   your progress, collection, coins and settings on the phone; we never had a
   copy.
2. **Delete the copies in your Google account.** Your Play Games save is
   deleted from **Google Play Games → Settings → Delete Play Games account &
   data**, where each game's data can be deleted on its own. Android backups
   are managed, and can be deleted, in your Google account's backup settings.
   Delete both before reinstalling, or the game will restore your progress.
3. **Stop anything new being collected.** In the app, open **Settings**, turn
   the Analytics switch off, and use the privacy options row to withdraw
   ads consent.
4. **Delete your advertising ID.** On Android, open **Settings → Google → Ads**
   (on some phones **Settings → Privacy → Ads**) and choose **Delete
   advertising ID**. Advertising data Google already holds is then no longer
   linked to your phone.
5. **Ask us anything about your data.** Email **gcapplicationdev@gmail.com**
   with the subject "Delete my data — Drop & Merge". The gameplay stats are
   anonymous totals with nothing that points to you, so there is no record of
   yours in them to delete. We reply within one month.

**What is deleted, and what is kept.** Your save file is deleted in step 1,
and its copies in your Google account in step 2.
Gameplay stats are anonymous totals, deleted automatically after 24 months.
Older versions of the app sent analytics to Google Firebase if you had opted
in; that data is held by Google and deleted at the end of its retention window.
Advertising data is held by Google, not by us, and is kept under Google's own
retention policy (https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention). We keep
nothing else.

## How long it is kept

Anonymous gameplay stats are kept for at most 24 months and then deleted
automatically. Advertising data is retained
by Google under its own advertising policies. Data stored on your device is kept
until you delete it in-app or uninstall the app. Copies of your progress in
your Google account are kept until you delete them (see *Deleting your data*).
Ideas you send us are kept while we use them to plan updates, and deleted at
the latest 24 months after they arrive.
